# Adsum 
  My greetings. This app offers you opportunity to change time usage for better organisation attendance of students. It will help every school, college and university to keep records of students' attendance and their grades(in future) in educational institutions. 

  It has 5-role's system:Student, Teacher, Admin, Cafeteria and Main Developer. Each one has different possibilities. For instance, admin can registrate new students and apply them to login in their school. Student himself can record his attend mark for any day. Main Developer can do everything except recording his attend mark.
